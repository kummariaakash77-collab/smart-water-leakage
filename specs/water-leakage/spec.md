# Water Leakage Detection Feature

## Overview

The Smart Water Leakage Detection System enables users to report, track, and monitor water leakage incidents.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1
Users shall be able to submit water leakage reports.

### Requirement 2
Users shall be able to view reported incidents.

### Requirement 3
Administrators shall be able to review and manage reports.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Users can create a report.
- Reports are stored successfully.
- Dashboard displays statistics.
- Administrators can review reports.
